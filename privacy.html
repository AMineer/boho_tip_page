Boho Tip Privacy Policy

Short version: your receipts never leave your phone.

Effective September 13, 2026

Boho Tip calculates tips and, if you use the receipt-scan feature, reads text off a photo entirely on your device using Apple's on-device Vision framework. We don't run any servers, we don't have an analytics vendor, and nothing you type, scan, or save is ever sent anywhere. This page explains exactly what that means.
What we collect Camera & Photos Where your data lives Purchases Third parties Children Changes Contact

01 What we collect

Nothing. Boho Tip does not collect, transmit, or have access to any data about you or your device. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics SDKs, and no network requests of any kind.

No accounts No analytics No ads No network access

02 Camera & Photos

If you tap Scan receipt, the app asks permission to use your camera or photo library so it can read a picture of your receipt. That image is handed to Apple's Vision framework, processed on your device to find the total, and then discarded — Boho Tip never saves the photo itself, and the image is never uploaded anywhere. You can decline camera or photo access entirely and still use every other part of the app by typing the bill in by hand.

03 Where your data lives

If you unlock Boho Tip Pro and choose to save a receipt, the amount, merchant name, tip, and date are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This never syncs to iCloud or to us — it stays on the device you saved it on. If you export your history to CSV, that file is handed to iOS's standard share sheet, and where it goes from there (Files, Mail, AirDrop, etc.) is entirely your choice.

04 Purchases

The one-time Boho Tip Pro unlock is processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We never see your payment details, billing address, or Apple ID — Apple's own privacy policy governs that transaction, not ours.

05 Third parties

Boho Tip doesn't share data with third parties, because it doesn't collect any to share. There are no embedded ad networks, analytics kits, or crash reporters.

06 Children's privacy

Boho Tip is a general-audience utility that collects no data from anyone, including children. It is not directed specifically at children under 13.

07 Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version adds optional iCloud sync — this page will be updated and the "Effective" date above will change accordingly. Given how the app works today, we don't expect that to happen often.

08 Contact

Questions about this policy or the app? Reach out at privacy@a2m4systems.com.
