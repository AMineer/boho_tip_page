Using the app

How does receipt scanning work?

Tap "Scan receipt," take a photo or pick one from your library, and Boho Tip reads the total using Apple's on-device Vision framework. It looks for lines like "Total" or "Amount Due" and fills the bill field automatically. If it can't find one confidently, it'll say so — just enter the amount by hand.

Is my receipt photo uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens on your device — no servers, no accounts, no network calls. See the Privacy Policy for the full picture.

Why can't I scan a receipt on the Simulator?

Camera access isn't available in Xcode's Simulator — this only affects testing, not real devices. On an actual iPhone, both camera and photo library work normally.

Boho Tip Pro

What does the Pro unlock include?

A one-time purchase (not a subscription) that unlocks: saved receipt history, CSV export for expense filing, and two extra color themes — Desert Sunset and Sage Meadow.

How do I export my receipts?

Open the history icon (top left of the calculator), tap the export icon in the top right, and share the CSV file anywhere — Files, Mail, AirDrop, or your expense software of choice.

I purchased Pro but it's not unlocking — what do I do?

Open the paywall (tap the history or theme icon) and tap "Restore Purchases." If that doesn't fix it, email us at support@a2m4systems.com with the email address on your Apple ID and we'll sort it out.
